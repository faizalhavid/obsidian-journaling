## 📐 Rule of Thumb Position Sizing

| Confidence | Max % Portfolio |
|------------|-----------------|
| Very High (9-10) | 15-20% |
| High (7-8) | 10-15% |
| Moderate (5-6) | 5-10% |
| Low (1-4) | Jangan beli / max 2-3% observation position |

**Max drawdown per position:** 15-25% (stop loss)

---

## 📊 Portfolio Review Cadence

- **Harian:** Cek IHSG, watchlist emiten (10 menit)
- **Mingguan:** Weekly Review — status portfolio, ide baru
- **Bulanan:** Rekap performa, revisit thesis
- **Kuartalan:** Deep review — hasil laporan keuangan, rebalance
- **Tahunan:** Strategy review — apakah pendekatan masih relevan?
