# Sektor Properti & Real Estate

---

## 1️⃣ Overview

- Sub-sektor: Residensial, Komersial (office/retail), Industrial, Hospitality
- Regulator: Kementerian ATR/BPN, PUPR, OJK (untuk REIT)
- Cyclical, sensitif terhadap BI Rate

---

## 2️⃣ Business Landscape

### Value Chain
1. **Landbank acquisition & permitting**
2. **Development & construction**
3. **Sales & marketing**
4. **Post-handover (recurring: rental, management)**

### Revenue Model
- Sales properti (project-based)
- Recurring: rental office / mall / hotel
- Land sales

## 6️⃣ Makro Sensitivity

- **BI Rate:** Very High — KPR appetite
- **GDP:** High — daya beli
- **Rupiah:** Medium — cost bahan bangunan import
- **Inflasi:** Medium

---

## 7️⃣ Outlook

**Bias:** Neutral to Positive (jika BI cut)

**Catalyst positif:**
- BI rate cut → KPR affordability
- Insentif pemerintah (PPN DTP)
- Recovery kelas menengah

**Risiko:**
- Kenaikan biaya konstruksi
- Slow moving inventory
- Debt refinancing
