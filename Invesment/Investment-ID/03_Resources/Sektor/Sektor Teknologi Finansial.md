## 5️⃣ Regulasi & Policy

- OJK Regulation 77/2016 tentang P2P Lending
- BI Regulation tentang Payment System
- QRIS mandate
- Consumer protection framework
- Data privacy (UU PDP)

**Risiko regulasi:** Perubahan cap interest rate, licensing baru, mandatory bank rupiah dsb.

---

## 6️⃣ Makro Sensitivity

- **BI Rate:** High sensitivity — untuk lending margin & consumer appetite
- **Rupiah:** Medium — dampak ke cost teknologi luar
- **GDP / consumption:** High — payment volume & lending demand
- **Inflasi:** Medium

---

## 7️⃣ Outlook 12-24 Bulan

**Bias:** Cautiously Positive

**Positif:**
- Path to profitability semakin dekat untuk player mature
- Regulatory framework yang lebih clear
- Kolaborasi bank-fintech makin dalam

**Negatif:**
- Kompetisi tetap intens
- Regulatory tightening potensial
- Consumer over-leverage risk (lending)

**Catalyst:**
- Earnings turnaround player utama
- M&A / IPO baru
- Sub-sektor breakthrough (crypto regulatory clarity, embedded finance)
