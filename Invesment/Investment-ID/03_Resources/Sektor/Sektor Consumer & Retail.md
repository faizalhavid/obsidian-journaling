# Sektor Consumer & Retail

---

## 1️⃣ Overview

- Sub-sektor: FMCG, F&B, Fashion, Home & Lifestyle, Retail Chain
- Regulator: BPOM, Kemendag, LPPOM MUI (halal)
- Defensive sector, sensitif ke daya beli

---

## 2️⃣ Business Landscape

### Format Retail
1. **Modern trade** — supermarket, hypermarket, convenience
2. **Traditional trade** — pasar tradisional, warung
3. **Specialty retail** — fashion, elektronik, home
4. **E-commerce & omnichannel**

### Value Chain
1. Sourcing / Manufacturing
2. Distribution
3. Retail (physical + digital)

## 5️⃣ Regulasi & Policy

- BPOM (Food & drug)
- Halal certification (mandatory 2024+)
- UMK / upah minimum
- Cukai (bagi tobacco, sugar-sweetened)

---

## 6️⃣ Makro Sensitivity

- **Inflasi:** High — cost & harga jual
- **BI Rate:** Medium — impact ke credit sales
- **GDP:** High — daya beli
- **Rupiah:** Medium — import content

---

## 7️⃣ Metrik Kunci Retail

- SSSG (Same Store Sales Growth) — pertumbuhan organik toko lama
- Store count / ekspansi
- Inventory turnover
- Gross margin trend
- Digital sales contribution

---

## 8️⃣ Outlook

**Bias:** Neutral

**Positif:**
- Middle class recovery
- Digital adoption meningkat
- Halal certification advantage untuk local player

**Negatif:**
- Inflasi tekanan daya beli
- E-commerce disruption toko fisik
- Cost pressure (bahan baku, upah)
