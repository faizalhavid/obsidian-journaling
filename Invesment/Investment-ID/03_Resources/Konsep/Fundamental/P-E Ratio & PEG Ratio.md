# P/E Ratio & PEG Ratio

> Price-to-Earnings: berapa kali laba per saham yang dibayar investor untuk membeli 1 saham.

---

## 🧭 Definisi

**PER (Price-to-Earnings Ratio):**
```
PER = Harga Saham / Earnings per Share (EPS)
```

**PEG (Price/Earnings to Growth):**
```
PEG = PER / (Growth EPS % per tahun)
```

PEG mengukur apakah PER sudah reasonable relatif terhadap growth-nya.

---

## 🧠 Kenapa Penting?

- Metrik valuasi paling populer & mudah dibandingkan
- Cepat untuk screening awal
- PEG membantu ketika membandingkan growth vs mature company

---

## 📊 Rule of Thumb

| PER | Interpretasi Umum |
|-----|-------------------|
| < 10 | Cheap (atau ada masalah struktural) |
| 10-15 | Reasonable untuk mature company |
| 15-25 | Fair-ish untuk growth company |
| > 25 | Mahal (harus punya growth solid) |

| PEG | Interpretasi |
|-----|--------------|
| < 1 | Undervalued relative to growth |
| 1 | Fair |
| > 1 | Overvalued relative to growth |

**Penting:** PER berbeda per sektor. Bandingkan dengan peers, jangan cross-sector.

---

## ⚠️ Pitfall

1. **EPS distorted by one-off items** — cek adjusted EPS
2. **Growth tidak sustainable** — PEG bisa menyesatkan bila growth 1x sekali
3. **Cyclical stock** — PER rendah di puncak siklus = overvalued
4. **Negative earnings** → PER tidak meaningful, pakai EV/Sales atau P/B
